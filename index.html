Orlando AdamsArchitecture and spatial design, for buildings and for film

I'm a final-year architecture student at Oxford Brookes. I design buildings around the people who use them, how they are made and how they feel to move through. Alongside architecture I work toward film and TV set design, where the same skills tell a story through space, light and atmosphere.

I'm looking for opportunities in architectural practice and in film and TV art departments. My strongest project, Set One Studios, brings both together: a fabrication hub in East London where sets, props and costumes are built for the studios next door.

Set One Studios

A fabrication hub for film and TV on the Malmesbury Estate in Bow, a short walk from Three Mills Studios. Sets, props and costumes are built here and fed into London's production network.

A three-storey hempcrete and timber workshop inside a polycarbonate shell. A skybridge links it to the existing block, which gains a workers' café, quieter offices, winter gardens and new community rooms.

The Set Design Court

The rooftop courts take the pitched and sawtooth rooflines of the surrounding streets and flatten them into 2D panels on perforated steel posts.

They come down as easily as they go up, and groups can reconfigure them without tools. The same logic as a flat on a sound stage.
